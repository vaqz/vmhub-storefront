Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

We want you to receive the item agreed upon. If an item is defective, damaged on arrival, incorrect or materially different from its confirmed description, contact us at katebaguio@gmail.com or +63 948 146 7951. Include your order details, explain the issue and provide photos if they help show the problem.

We will review the concern and explain the appropriate next steps. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, the remedy may include repair, replacement or refund. We will explain any return or inspection arrangements before asking you to send an item back.

For model-specific accessories, please check your exact phone model and product compatibility before confirming your order. This does not remove your rights if we supply the wrong item or materially misrepresent its compatibility.

For a change-of-mind request where the item is correctly supplied and has no defect, contact us before returning it. Acceptance of change-of-mind returns depends on the circumstances and applicable law; it is not automatically guaranteed. Do not send an item back without contacting us first.

Where a refund is due, we will confirm the refund method and explain the expected processing steps. Return shipping and other charges will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the case.
