1. Ordering and price confirmation

Customers can submit an order request through the website and WhatsApp. A submitted request is not an accepted order, reservation, or payment. VM Hub will confirm the exact item, phone-model compatibility, stock, final price, delivery fee, and expected delivery date before accepting an order.

Prices displayed in the catalog are indicative estimates until confirmed by VM Hub. No payment should be made based only on an unconfirmed request. A confirmed order should state the items, quantities, final item prices, delivery fee, total amount, delivery method, and any applicable warranty or return terms.

2. Delivery, courier shipping, pickup and sourcing

Local delivery — Butuan City

Local delivery may be requested within Butuan City. The service area, delivery fee, and estimated arrival time will be confirmed based on the address, item availability, and delivery arrangements. No same-day or fixed-time delivery is guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed.

Nationwide courier shipping

Customers elsewhere in the Philippines may request courier shipping. Courier coverage, shipping fee, dispatch timing, and estimated transit time depend on the destination, parcel, and courier. These details will be confirmed before order acceptance. Courier estimates are not guarantees and may be affected by weather, holidays, or operational delays.

Store pickup

Pickup may be requested, but the pickup location, schedule, and item readiness must be confirmed by VM Hub before the customer visits. A request is not a reservation or confirmation that an item is ready.

Items sourced from suppliers

Customers may request products that are not currently in stock. VM Hub will check supplier availability and then communicate the confirmed item specification, final price, any required lead time, and delivery or pickup options. Any estimate must be realistic and based on supplier and logistics information; it is not a promise until confirmed. If the item cannot be sourced or the terms are not acceptable, the customer may decline the offer.

Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address and reachable contact number. Please inspect the parcel upon receipt and contact VM Hub promptly if the item is incorrect, damaged, or missing. Where practical, keep the packaging and proof of purchase while a concern is reviewed. Fees and timing are confirmed before order acceptance.

4. Privacy notice

VM Hub collects only information needed to respond to inquiries and process order requests, such as name, mobile number, email (if provided), delivery address, selected products, compatibility details, and order-related messages.

Information is used to confirm stock and prices, communicate about orders, arrange delivery, provide after-sales support, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. Order requests sent through WhatsApp are also processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own terms and privacy practices.

We do not ask customers to submit passwords, PINs, one-time passwords, or full payment-card credentials through the order form. Information may be shared with a courier or service provider only when needed to fulfill an order, or when required or permitted by law. Access should be limited to people who need it for business operations.

Customers may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information, or to ask a privacy question, subject to applicable legal requirements. Contact: katebaguio@gmail.com. This notice should be updated if the store adds analytics, online accounts, payment processors, or other data collection tools.
